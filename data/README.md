## Energy Calculation: From Power (kW) to Hourly Energy (kWh)

### Why Global Active Power is in kW
In the raw UCI dataset, `Global_active_power` represents household minute-averaged active power in kilowatts (kW), which is an instantaneous rate of energy consumption.

### How Hourly Energy (kWh) is Calculated
To convert minute-level power readings into energy over an hour:
1. Each 1-minute interval of power $P_m$ (kW) represents energy consumption:
   $$\text{energy\_minute (kWh)} = \frac{P_m}{60}$$
2. Over a fully observed 60-minute hour, total energy is the Riemann sum:
   $$\text{energy\_kwh} = \sum_{m=1}^{60} \frac{P_m}{60} = \bar{P} \times 1.0\text{ hour}$$
   where $\bar{P}$ is the mean power in kW over the hour.

---

##  Missing Data, Incomplete Hours, and Duplicate Policies

### 1. Hourly Completeness & Coverage Policy (`min_hourly_coverage=0.90`)
- **Threshold**: Requires at least 90% valid minute readings (i.e., $\ge 54$ valid minutes out of 60) for an hour to be accepted.
- **Within-Hour Scaling**: For qualifying hours ($N \ge 54$), missing readings are assumed to occur at random and represented by the hour's observed mean power:
  $$\text{energy\_kwh} = \bar{P}_{\text{valid}} \times 1.0\text{ hour} = \left(\frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N P_i\right) \times 1.0$$
  This prevents missing minutes within an otherwise complete hour from being falsely treated as zero consumption.
- **Exclusion of Incomplete & Missing Hours**: Hours with $< 90\%$ coverage or zero observations are completely excluded from the dataset rather than converted to zero consumption.

### 2. Duplicate Timestamp Resolution Policy
- When multiple observations share the same minute timestamp:
  - Non-conflicting duplicates: Consolidated to a single minute observation.
  - Conflicting duplicates: Flagged in the audit, and deterministically resolved by taking the arithmetic mean of all valid readings for that minute timestamp.
  - Deduplication ensures duplicate records cannot artificially inflate the minute count or distort coverage.

## Running Preprocessing

To run the data loading and preprocessing pipeline:

```bash
# Default paths (reads data/raw/household_power_consumption.txt, writes data/processed/hourly_energy.csv)
python main.py --stage preprocess

# Custom parameters:
python main.py --stage preprocess --raw-data-path data/raw/household_power_consumption.txt --processed-data-path data/processed/hourly_energy.csv --min-hourly-coverage 0.90
```

## Processed Output Schema

The generated `data/processed/hourly_energy.csv` adheres to:

| Column | Type | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `timestamp` | `datetime64[ns]` | Hour-start timestamp (e.g., `2006-12-16 17:00:00`) |
| `energy_kwh` | `float64` | Estimated energy consumption during the hour in kilowatt-hours |
| `valid_minutes` | `int64` | Count of unique, valid minute observations within the hour |
| `coverage_ratio` | `float64` | Ratio of valid minutes to expected minutes (`valid_minutes / 60.0`) |
